# Outreach Package — Index

Communication materials for the CRC metagenomics paper.

**Authors**: Alejandro Velazquez (Berkeley CS undergrad, primary author) and Rachel Selbrede (biology collaborator).

All files in this directory are non-technical or semi-technical communication assets. They are *not* part of the paper itself (which lives in `manuscript/`) or the conference submission (`conference/`, `submission/`).

---

## When to use each file

| File | Length | Audience | Use when |
|------|--------|----------|----------|
| `lay_summary_50w.md` | 50 words | General public | Twitter bio, homepage tagline, one-line abstract for non-scientists |
| `lay_summary_200w.md` | 200 words | College-educated non-scientist | Journal "plain language summary" field, hospital comms office, lab website |
| `twitter_thread.md` | 10 tweets | Twitter/X (researchers + science-curious public) | Post-acceptance or post-preprint announcement on social media |
| `linkedin_post.md` | ~300 words | LinkedIn (professional network, recruiters, biotech industry) | Post-preprint announcement; also useful as a self-introduction when applying to PhD programs or jobs |
| `press_release_draft.md` | ~500 words | University PR office, science journalists | Hand to Berkeley CS communications team; they will adapt boilerplate and add real contacts before distribution |
| `blog_post_long.md` | ~1,800 words | Personal site, lab site, Substack, Hacker News | Long-form storytelling — first-person narrative of the project, including the country-aware LODO discovery story |
| `elevator_pitch.md` | 30s / 60s / 2min | Spoken | Conference receptions, poster sessions, casual networking, interviews |
| `qa_for_journalists.md` | 10 Q&A | Science journalists | Prep document before any interview; also useful to share with the PR office alongside the press release |

---

## Recommended workflow

**At preprint posting (bioRxiv / arXiv):**
1. Post `twitter_thread.md` (replace figure paths with uploaded images and the link placeholder with the preprint URL).
2. Post `linkedin_post.md`.
3. Publish `blog_post_long.md` on personal site / Substack and link it from the tweets.
4. Email `lay_summary_200w.md` + the preprint link to anyone whose name appears in your acknowledgments.

**At journal submission:**
1. Use `lay_summary_200w.md` for the journal's plain-language summary field (most journals now require this).
2. Use `lay_summary_50w.md` for the short author summary if the journal asks for one.

**At journal acceptance / publication:**
1. Send `press_release_draft.md` and `qa_for_journalists.md` to the Berkeley CS communications office at least two weeks before the publication date. Let them edit boilerplate, add the real contact info, and coordinate timing with the journal's embargo.
2. Re-post the Twitter thread and LinkedIn post with the final journal URL.
3. Update the blog post with the publication link.

**For any interview, podcast, or panel:**
1. Skim `elevator_pitch.md` for whichever time slot fits.
2. Skim `qa_for_journalists.md` for likely questions.

## Style and tone choices baked into this package

- **No overclaiming.** AUC 0.78 is explicitly *not* described as a clinical-grade test anywhere. Every piece distinguishes "promising signal" from "ready for the clinic."
- **No statistical jargon in lay materials.** No "AUC," "p-value," "DeLong," "bootstrap" in the 50-word, 200-word, press release, or Q&A. Those terms appear only in the blog post (briefly) and the Twitter thread (where the audience expects them).
- **First-person voice in LinkedIn, blog, and elevator pitch.** Reflects Alex's actual position as a Berkeley CS undergrad on a first paper — curious, technically careful, not posturing as senior.
- **Rachel's biology contribution is credited explicitly** in the LinkedIn post, the press release, and the blog post (the oral-pathobiont interpretation).
- **The work is framed as a methodological refinement**, not a brand-new biomarker discovery. The country-aware LODO fix and the negative pathway result are the two stories told most prominently.
