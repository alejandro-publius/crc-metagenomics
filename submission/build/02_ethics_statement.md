# Ethics Statement

This study used exclusively publicly available, fully de-identified human
gut metagenomic data distributed through the `curatedMetagenomicData`
Bioconductor package. All original cohorts obtained institutional review
board / ethics committee approval and written informed consent from
participants prior to data deposition; these approvals are documented
in the primary publications cited for each cohort (FengQ_2015,
GuptaA_2019, ThomasAM_2018a, ThomasAM_2018b, ThomasAM_2019_c,
VogtmannE_2016, WirbelJ_2018, YachidaS_2019, YuJ_2015, ZellerG_2014).

Because this secondary analysis did not involve any identifiable
individuals and did not generate new human-subjects data, no additional
IRB review was required under [Institution] policy and the U.S. Common
Rule (45 CFR 46.102(e)). Per-sample metadata used in this study are
limited to study identifier, condition (CRC, adenoma, control), age,
sex, BMI, and country, all as released in `curatedMetagenomicData`.

# Consent for Publication

Not applicable. No identifiable individual-level data are reported.

# Animal Research

Not applicable. No animal subjects were involved.
